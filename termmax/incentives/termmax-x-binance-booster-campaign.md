## About TermMax

[TermMax](https://app-v2.termmax.ts.finance/) is a fixed-rate borrowing/lending and options trading protocol built for people who want to know exactly what they are paying and exactly when it ends. Instead of floating rates that reprice block by block, every TermMax market has a fixed rate and a fixed maturity. Known rate, known term, known risk.

TermMax is live across Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, Berachain, HyperEVM, Robinhood Chain and more, with leveraged fixed-rate positions, curated vaults for lenders, and TermPrime for institutional term financing.

## FAQ

#### **Q: What is TermMax?**

A: TermMax is a fixed-rate borrowing/lending and options trading protocol. Lenders get a known yield for a known term, borrowers get a rate that cannot drift, and options traders can size a position knowing their cost of carry up front. It runs on Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, Berachain, HyperEVM, Robinhood Chain and more.

#### **Q: Who can join the campaign?**

A: Anyone with a Binance Keyless Wallet and at least 2 Binance Alpha Points. Joining deducts 2 Alpha Points.

#### Q: Are any countries or regions restricted?

A: No regions are restricted by this campaign. Binance Wallet's own terms of service still apply.

#### Q: How do rewards work?

A: Each part has its own reward pool. The lucky draw pool is distributed to up to 80,000 winners. The Binance Square pool is split equally among 1,000 leaderboard winners.

#### Q: Do I have to complete all five tasks for the lucky draw?

A: Yes. All five must be completed and verified to enter the lucky draw. Partial completion does not qualify, and doing extra tasks does not increase your odds within this pool.

#### Q: When will I receive rewards?

A: Rewards are distributed and unlocked on the $TMX TGE day.

#### Q: Is there a cap on participants?

A: No. Everyone eligible can participate, but only 80,000 users can win from the lucky draw pool and only 1,000 from the Binance Square pool.

#### Q: Do I need to hold $TMX or deposit funds to join?

A: No. All tasks are free to complete and open to eligible Binance Keyless Wallet users. Connecting your wallet to the TermMax app does not require a deposit.

#### Q: Where can I check if my task is verified?

A: After hitting "Verify" inside the campaign page, you will see a confirmation message for that task.

#### Q: My task is not verifying. What should I do?

A: Make sure the action was completed with the same account you are verifying, wait a few minutes, then try again. If it still fails, reach out in our Discord.

#### Q: Who can I contact for help?

A: Join our[ Discord community](https://discord.ts.finance) and open a ticket for support.
